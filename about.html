NAM JUNG KIM IS AN ARTIST WORKING ACROSS INSTALLATION, VIDEO, RESEARCH-BASED PRACTICE, DRAWING AND PAINTING. HER WORK EXPLORES RELATIONSHIPS BETWEEN ENVIRONMENT, MATERIAL, AND BUILT SPACE THROUGH SPATIAL INTERVENTIONS AND MOVING IMAGE. HER PRACTICE OFTEN COMBINES ARTISTIC RESEARCH, SITE-SPECIFIC INSTALLATION, AND COMMUNITY-ORIENTED PROJECTS.
